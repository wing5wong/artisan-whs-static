Welcome to Whanganui High School! 🌏💙

We're excited to welcome our newest international and exchange students from Belgium, Brazil, Czech Republic, Denmark, Germany, Italy, Japan, and Vietnam to our school community.

Over the coming months, you'll make new friends, experience Kiwi life, challenge yourselves, and create memories that will last a lifetime. We hope you'll embrace every opportunity, both inside and outside the classroom, and make Whanganui your home away from home.

We wish you all an unforgettable experience and can't wait to see the incredible contributions you'll make to our school community.

Welcome to the WHS whānau!
