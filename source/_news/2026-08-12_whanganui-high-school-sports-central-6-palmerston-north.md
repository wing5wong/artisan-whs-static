On Tuesday 11 August, WHS headed to Palmerston North for the annual Junior Central 6 Tournament — and what a day it was! 🔥

WHS came away with six wins, taking out:

🏆 Boys Rugby

🏆 Boys Basketball

🏆 Boys Football

🏆 Girls Football

🏆 Netball

🏆 Boys Hockey

🥈 Girls Hockey — 2nd Place

With all sports except hockey being played at the Central Energy Trust Arena, it was awesome having everyone together and being able to get around and support each other throughout the day.

A huge thank you to Larson Whittaker for capturing the day through his photography, to all our coaches for their time and commitment, and to the senior students who came along to referee and umpire.

Apologies for the lack of hockey photos — their games were held at a separate venue. I’ll put some photos up once I track some down!

What a day for WHS!
