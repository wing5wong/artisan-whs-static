On Wednesday, Whanganui Rugby hosted a Fast 5 Rippa Tournament at Springvale Park, with WHS entering two girls' teams and five boys' teams.

It was a fantastic day of fun, teamwork and great rugby! A special congratulations to our Senior Boys, who took out the U18 grade, and our Junior Boys, who claimed the U15 title.

Well done to all our players for getting out there, giving it their all and representing WHS so well! 🏉👏
