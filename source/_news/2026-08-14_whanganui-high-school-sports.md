Well done to our WHS Girls 1st XI Hockey team, who took out the Manawatu competition last night! 🏆

After a hard-fought final finished 0–0, the girls held their nerve to secure the win on a penalty count. What a way to finish the competition!

We are so proud of you all and the determination, teamwork and effort you have shown throughout the season.

A huge thank you to Coach Luke Kirk and Manager Mr Grant Hardy for all your time, support and commitment to the team.

Congratulations, girls — an outstanding achievement! 💚💛

All the very best for Winter Tournament Week. Go well and represent WHS with everything you’ve got! 🏑🏆
