Another fantastic result for Denby Whittington at the weekend’s second round of the NZ Downhill Championships! With five rounds in total, the competition is well underway.

Denby produced an absolutely scorching final run, clocking an impressive 2:25 to secure 1st place in the U17 Boys. Even more impressive, his time was the second fastest of the day across all age groups!

This is a huge achievement for Denby, who has been building towards this with some great recent results and countless hours of hard work on the bike. It’s fantastic to see that effort paying off!

Congratulations, Denby! What a result! 💛💚
