🏃‍♂️🧭 Orienteering Success for WHS! 🧭🏃‍♂️

Today, two WHS teams competed at the Manawatū Schools Orienteering Teams Championships, with some fantastic results!

🥇 1st Place – Junior Boys: Bruce McGregor, Sean Frieslaar and Xavier Brotherton

🏅 4th Place – Junior Boys: George McGregor, Levi Standen and Dexter Brotherton

An especially impressive achievement was that both teams successfully found every control, earning the highest combined score across all grades! 👏🔥

Congratulations, boys — a fantastic effort and a great result for WHS!
