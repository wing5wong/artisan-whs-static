A huge thank you to everyone who took part in Pool 4 School this year! It has been fantastic to see everyone enjoying the games, developing their skills, and improving throughout the season — especially your stance, grip and bridge.

🏆 Main Champion: Ryan James

Mr Ding hopes to see you all back around the table again next year! 🎱
