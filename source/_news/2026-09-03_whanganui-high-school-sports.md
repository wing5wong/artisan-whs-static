## **Lower North Island Secondary Schools Netball's Post**

It is our pleasure to award our 2nd Service Award for 2026. Congratulations to Lisa Murphy from Whanganui High School. To be able to award her her Service Award in front of the team she has coached for many many years made it that much more special.

We look forward to seeing you here for many more years Lisa. Whanganui High School is lucky to have you supporting their young netballers.

**Whanganui High School**

Yet another special achievement for the amazing Lisa Murphy.
