Great day for the Equestrian Team

Lily McIntyre on Woodrow Lillian gained 1st, 2nd and 2nd for her rounds and was awarded 90cm-1m Champion

Overall our team was awarded the Competition Runner Ups (just 2 points short of the winners) 💚💛💚
