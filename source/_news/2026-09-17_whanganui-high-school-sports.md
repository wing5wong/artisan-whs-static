Congratulations to our WHS hockey players Amy Davidson, Cody Davidson, Deejai O'Brien, Troy Davidson who represented Wanganui at National U18s Tournament. They have been selected for the Central Falcons U18s camp, which is being held in Waiarapa from the 26th - 28th Sept. 

From that camp a squad will be selected to play in the FHL- Future Hockey League against the 3 other franchises the Alpiners, Mavericks and Tridents

Where they will then select the NZ U18s junior black stick teams.
