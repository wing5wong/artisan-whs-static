🏆 WHS SPORTS AWARDS 2026 – OUR WINNERS! 🏆

Now that the dust has settled after an incredible evening celebrating sport at Whanganui High School, it’s time to share the results with our wider community and recognise all of our amazing award winners! 🎉👏

With over 340 people filling the Eulogy Lounge at the Whanganui Racecourse, it was fantastic to see so many people come together to celebrate the outstanding achievements, dedication and commitment of our sportsmen and sportswomen throughout the year. The evening ran smoothly, and it was a wonderful opportunity to acknowledge the hard work and success of our students, coaches and sporting community.

A huge thank you to everyone who helped make the evening such a success. There are far too many people to name individually, but you know who you are, and your time, effort and support are truly appreciated! 💚💛

To all our award winners, congratulations! You have worked hard to achieve what you have, and it was fantastic to celebrate your successes. We are excited to share your achievements with the wider Whanganui community.

🏆 Congratulations to all our 2026 WHS Sports Awards winners! 💚💛
