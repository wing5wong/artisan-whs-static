Outstanding Achievement in Design!

A huge congratulations to international student, Manh Chau Anh (Lemon) Nguyen from Viet Nam, who was awarded the WHS Coombs Cup for Design in Year 12 at last Wednesday's Cultural Awards Evening!

This is a significant achievement and a wonderful recognition of Lemon's exceptional creativity, talent and hard work in Design.

Whanganui High School has a strong reputation for excellence in the Visual Arts, and receiving this award is something Lemon can be very proud of.

It's fantastic to see our international students achieving at such a high level and making their mark in the wider school community.

Well done, Lemon! A very well-deserved award! 💚💛
