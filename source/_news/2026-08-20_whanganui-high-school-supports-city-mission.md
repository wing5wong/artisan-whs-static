Over the past few weeks students at Whanganui High Schoolhave been collecting non-perishable items to help City Mission take care ofpeople in our city who may need some help this Winter.

Last Friday it all came together when senior students spearheaded by the Community Prefects (who had managed the campaign) and HouseLeaders got together to sort, pack account and load over 8600 cans and othergoods.

To generate a buzz of excitement, Whanganui High School runsthe event as a House competition, and there was much interest in the finalresults: 4th- Awa; 3rd – Whenua; 2nd-Maunga; 1st– Moana. There was so much generosity shown by so many, and we are grateful toRite Price Rentals for lending us a van at the very last minute because we hadso much  to ship out. Thank you to themany friends and whanau who gave so generously. Olivia Wilson and Xander Booth(Heads of the Community Prefect team) were thrilled with the results and wereso happy that Whanganui High School had pulled together so magnificently tohelp our community.
