Students in Years 10, 11, and 12 get to make subject choices for next year.  

Each Faculty has a display in the library and students are able to speak to staff about the subjects offered.  

Students in Year 9 will are able to have a look but they will not be selecting subjects till Term 4.

We also have an Information Evening for parents on Tuesday 11 August in the Library from 5.00 - 6.30pm.
